FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Still wondering about something? Give us a call and we'll talk it through.

Do you need to see my property first?

No. We can give you a valid price and options from a few questions, and we've worked on every type of house in Adelaide.

If you accept the price, we can often quote and install on the same day. If we can't find a suitable space, there's no charge.

Do I need council approval?

No. Using your roof space for storage doesn't need council approval. We don't alter any structural elements. We only add treated timber.

How long does an install take?

A standard ladder and storage floor takes around 4–5 hours. Ceiling insulation is usually installed in a day.

What can I store up there?

Clothing, boxes, sports equipment, camping gear and more. If it fits through the hatch, it can go in your new storage space.

What materials do you use?

H2 treated timber for the battens and framing, and 19mm termite-treated particleboard for the floor. We also offer a range of carefully selected, quality attic ladders.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Marion and install across Adelaide.
